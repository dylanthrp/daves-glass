What we do

Three trades, one shop.

A full-service glass shop in Caro, Michigan. We handle commercial glass, residential windows and doors, custom shower enclosures, and auto glass replacement — under one roof, with one phone number to call.

Commercial

Commercial Glass

Storefronts, office partitions, entrance doors, safety glass, and security glazing. We work with builders, property managers, and business owners across the Thumb — new construction and replacement.

Typical work: Storefronts · entrance doors · office partitions · tempered & laminated safety glass · security glazing

Residential

Window replacement, broken-glass repair, patio doors, storm doors, and the kind of small jobs other shops won't come out for. We work on homes from Caro to Bad Axe to Lapeer.

Typical work: Window replacement · broken-glass repair · patio doors · storm doors · insulating glass units

Shower Enclosures

Custom-measured, custom-cut frameless and semi-frameless glass enclosures. We template your space, fabricate the panels, and install them in one trip. Showroom-quality results, installed in a single morning.

Typical work: Frameless enclosures · semi-frameless · sliding glass doors · custom angles · heavy glass

Auto Glass

Auto Glass

Windshield replacement, chip repair, and side & back glass for all makes and models. We work directly with most insurance companies and can usually get you back on the road the same day.

Typical work: Windshield replacement · chip & crack repair · side glass · back glass · insurance claims welcome

Local & family-owned

Three generations, one shop, one number.

Dave started this shop in 1978. Today his family still runs it, and the same families that called him in the '80s still call us. We don't franchise, we don't subcontract, and we don't upsell you on things you don't need. If a window can be repaired instead of replaced, we'll tell you.

We work in Tuscola, Huron, Sanilac, and Lapeer counties — from our shop in Caro out to Bad Axe, Sandusky, Marlette, and Lapeer. If you're not sure whether you're in our area, just call.
